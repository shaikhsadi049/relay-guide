Introduction

Gold is not the market
it was ten years ago.

Since 2024 and 2025 gold has moved in long, violent, news-charged waves, a regime that punishes robots built on the calm ranges of the last decade. Relay was built the other way around: designed, tuned and validated on the modern market first, using real tick data, with its protections set for the days the market stops cooperating.

Inside, three engines share one account. A trend engine rides the big waves with an adaptive trail, a fast engine banks short bursts, and a quiet-market engine works the hours in between. The measured condition of the market decides which of them may act.

No strategy works forever, and anyone who promises otherwise is selling a story. An edge belongs to a market regime, not to the laws of nature. Relay's edge was measured on gold from 2025 onward. If that regime changes, the results will change with it, and Relay will be re-validated for the new one. Every number in this guide is a test measurement under stated conditions, including the losing ones.

Install and first attach

Five minutes, and nothing to load: the tested configuration is compiled in.

  1. Open an XAUUSD chart, under whatever name your broker gives gold, on any timeframe. Relay reads the timeframes it needs by itself.
  2. Attach Relay. Every default you see in the dialog is the tested value, so on a fresh chart there is nothing to change. If you ran an earlier version of Relay, press Reset in the Inputs tab first: MetaTrader keeps the old values otherwise.
  3. Enable Algo Trading in the terminal and allow it in the EA's Common tab. The dashboard appears within seconds.
  4. Check the dollar gauge. Relay reads your broker's EURUSD as a dollar-confirmation gauge and finds its name by itself. If the Experts log says the dollar dial is off, your broker names it with a suffix or prefix (EURUSD.r, EURUSDm and so on): type that exact name into Dollar gauge symbol.
  5. Leave it alone. One chart, and nothing else trading the account: no manual trades on the same symbol and no second EA beside it.
Upgrading?

The tested configuration lives inside the EA, not in a side file. Upgrading is the one moment that needs care: press Reset in the Inputs tab, because MetaTrader silently carries your old values into the new version.

How it trades

Three engines, and the market picks

Gold does not behave the same way every week, so Relay does not trade it the same way.

The trend engine

Rides the move

Joins a sustained move and gives the winner room to run, with an adaptive trail that locks profit as the trade proves itself.

BOLT

The fast engine

When a move lacks conviction it takes the smaller, quicker profit instead of waiting for a target that is not coming.

COVE

The quiet-market engine

Works the conditions the trend engine sits out, instead of forcing a trend trade into a market with no trend in it.

Only one trend position is open at a time. There is no grid, no martingale, no averaging down and no recovery multiplier. Every trade is sent with its stop loss in the same request, so no position ever sits on the server without one.

The stop distance follows the volatility the market is actually showing: wider in a storm, tighter in calm conditions. BOLT and COVE can each be switched off, and the input dialog says what that costs before you do it.

How it trades

How the lot size is decided

A staircase, not a smooth curve, and never a function of the last trade's result.

With the default Risk profile sizing, the lot is exactly:

lot = floor( balance ÷ step ) × minimum lot × signal scale

The minimum lot is your broker's smallest gold lot, 0.01 on most accounts. On the default High profile the step is 500: a balance of 500 to 999 trades 0.01, 1,000 trades 0.02, 1,500 trades 0.03, and so on. Moving from 999 to 1,000 doubles the lot in one step, which is the most common reason a jump gets noticed.

ProfileBalance per minimum lot
Super Low10,000
Low5,000
Medium1,000
High (default, very high risk)500 in calm conditions, 400 in a storm
Customyour own step

In a storm the High step tightens from 500 to 400, so the same balance sits one notch higher: at 2,000 that is 0.04 in calm conditions and 0.05 in a storm, with no change in balance at all. This is deliberate and was measured, not assumed.

Signal scale. Full agreement across three timeframes trades at 100% of that size, two of three at 60%, one of three at 40%, and BOLT's quick trades at 50%. None of this scaling can act while the lot is already at the broker's minimum: half of 0.01 is still 0.01. Near the 500 minimum most trades therefore run at a flat 0.01, and the conviction scaling only becomes real above about 1,000. That is the honest reason the recommended balance is 1,000 rather than the 500 floor.

A broker whose gold minimum is larger than 0.01

The ladder counts in your broker's minimum lot. Where that minimum is 0.1, the same balance trades ten times the size shown above. Check your symbol's minimum volume before you trade, or use Fixed lot or Percent-of-balance risk sizing.

This is not martingale

Martingale means increasing size after a loss to win it back. The result of the previous trade is not an input to the formula at any point. Size grows only as the account grows, and it steps back down the same staircase if the balance falls. You can check this yourself: set Detailed log to true, and every trade prints a line such as LOT: 0.04 lots | SL 12.35 | risk 2.10% of 2000.00. A trade taken straight after a loss and one taken after a win, at the same balance, carry the same lot.

If you would rather have no steps at all, set How the lot size is decided to Fixed lot for a constant size, or to Percent-of-balance risk for smooth scaling with the stop distance.

How it trades

Built for today's gold market

Relay was built and validated on the XAUUSD market regime of 2025 onward, which is why every backtest in this guide starts on 1 January 2025. Earlier years may test flat or negative, and Relay says so: whenever the Strategy Tester runs a period before 2025, a notice appears on the chart and in the log.

If gold's market regime changes, Relay will be re-validated for the new one.

The protections described below limit how fast damage can happen in a market Relay was not built for. They cannot turn a hostile market into a profitable one.

Protection

Risk control

Hard risk cap, 5%

Skips, never shrinks

A trade whose stop would risk more than the set percentage of the balance is not taken. It is not quietly made smaller to fit.

Loss brakes, 3% / 6%

Daily and weekly

Trading stops for the rest of the day, or week, after the set loss, and resumes in the next period.

Weekend

Flat before the gap

Relay reads your broker's own published Friday close and goes flat five hours before it, so no position is carried through the Sunday gap, whatever time zone the server runs in.

Every order

Checked before it is sent

An entry the account cannot carry, or whose stops the broker would refuse, is skipped and the reason written to the log, rather than sent to be rejected.

Protection

The news filter

Off by default, and forced on in prop mode. It reads MetaTrader 5's built-in economic calendar, so Relay itself makes no internet request of any kind. You choose the currencies (USD and EUR by default), high impact only or medium and high, how many minutes to block before and after an event (30 and 30), and whether an open trade is closed before the event.

With the filter on, the dashboard shows the next high-impact event: its name from your broker's calendar, the wait in total minutes with the same span in brackets (2285 min (1d 14h) is one number, not two), and the forecast and previous figures. Inside an event's blackout window the card switches to a red HIGH IMPACT NOW line.

In the Strategy Tester

The MT5 calendar is not available inside the tester, so a backtest runs without the news filter. The filter was measured off by default for retail accounts: always-on filtering cost more than it saved.

Protection

Prop firm mode

One switch reshapes Relay for funded-account rules.

Turning on Prop mode changes three things at once: sizing becomes 0.35% risk per trade, the position count is held at one, and the news filter is forced on, including closing an open trade before the news. News gaps are the one thing an equity guardian cannot outrun.

A guardian then watches your firm's daily loss limit and total drawdown limit, with a safety buffer, flattens everything before a limit can be touched, and halts for the day, or for good, if its floors are reached. The dashboard's prop card shows the three numbers a funded trader lives by: Day loss room, Total DD room, and Guardian (Armed, Day halt, HALTED).

The bad case is just as real

On the default very high risk settings, equity fell 23% below its peak in 2025 and 29% in 2026, inside years that both ended in profit. If that depth is more than you want, choose a lower profile: Medium trades about half the size of High on the same balance, Low about a tenth. The shape stays the same; the depth scales.

A run of several losing trades in a row is ordinary operation, and single losing months happen. The trend engine's best trades historically come soon after losses, which is why Relay does not stop after a losing streak.

A lasting change in gold's character is the real risk. The protections limit how fast the damage can happen; no robot turns a hostile regime into profit. Run a demo first, and judge Relay on months, not days.

Reference

Your first weeks

The part most vendors leave out: what you see first is a handful of trades, and that is a different experience from a year.

Many small results

Plenty of trades close near zero

The fast engine takes most of the trades and produces a small share of the money, while a handful of trend trades carry the year. Expect a screen full of small results.

Scratches

Profit that comes back

A trade can show real open profit, have its stop moved to break-even, and still close at almost nothing. It is the price of leaving the big winners alone: every tested way to rescue those scratches cost more on the runners than it saved.

Timing

When you attach matters

Your first impression is decided largely by when you start, not by the robot. A single trade can carry a month, and someone who attaches a fortnight later may meet only the quiet stretch that follows.

Judgement

Judge at 60 days, not 8 trades

If a fortnight of small scratches and a couple of stop-outs would make you switch it off, run it on demo for two months first. That decision is better made before your money is involved.
