Before you attach it

  • Symbol: XAUUSD, under whatever name your broker gives it. Two-digit and three-digit gold contracts are both handled.
  • Timeframe: any. The engines read the timeframes they need for themselves.
  • Account: hedging or netting, any balance.
  • No DLLs, no WebRequest, no external dependency. The news filter reads a local file, Relay_News.csv, from the terminal's Files folder.
